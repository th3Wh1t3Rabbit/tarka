# What the evidence can and cannot say

**A transfer records movement, not motive.** Its fields can establish a recorded amount, token, time, sender, receiver, and transaction reference. They do not name the person controlling an address.

**A case nickname is a reading aid.** First Engine and Receiving Vault refer to addresses in the narrative. They are not claims about an entity’s real identity or a provider’s private label.

**Several records can describe one event.** Do not sum overlapping views into additional value moved. Do not treat two views as independent corroboration without checking their origin.

**A missing value is unknown, not zero.** A query with no matches in a bounded collection is not proof of absence across a whole chain.

**A supporting record is not the checked transaction proof.** A shared hash can connect views, but evidence class and exact fields remain separate.

**A snapshot is not a live quotation.** Euler’s historical window and the later BRCG market snapshot answer different questions. Neither refreshes when someone opens the game.

**Fiction does not supply evidence.** Rook’s inventory, a handwritten balance, and Arthur’s dialogue motivate investigation; they cannot establish ownership or an onchain balance by themselves.

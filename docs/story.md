# Welcome to the Records Office

Rook has been summoned to investigate the Euler Finance breach. Unfortunately, his case brief is somewhere else, his blockchain knowledge is mostly enthusiasm, and the only person who can help him takes office procedure extremely seriously.

Arthur the Archivist expected a top-tier investigator. He got you.

What follows is a pixel-art, early-’90s-style point-and-click quest through paperwork, peculiar possessions, and an unusually expensive trail of receipts. Talk your way through a frosty welcome, rummage where you are permitted, and approach the Nansen terminal with the one thing an ocean of data cannot supply on its own: a useful question.

Tarka mixes dry exchanges, visual reactions, bureaucracy jokes, and crypto-flavoured asides with a real historical investigation. Newcomers learn alongside Rook; experienced crypto readers will recognise a few familiar habits, phrases, and traps. You do not need to know how to operate an API to enjoy the adventure.

This is not a fictional exploit wrapped in real-looking numbers. The office and its characters are invented, but the selected transaction fields and the limits of the conclusion are retained from the evidence. Your task is to connect what the records establish—not to guess the identity of a hidden villain.

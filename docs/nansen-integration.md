# Nansen integration and evidence boundaries

Nansen-derived historical data drives the investigation rather than appearing
as decoration. It determines the available questions, candidate records,
contradiction, falsifier, comparison path, and exact receipt used to close the
Euler case.

The shipped browser does not contact Nansen. It reads a frozen, reviewed,
public-safe competition corpus from `public/scenarios/`. This makes play
deterministic, keyless, and reproducible while keeping provider credentials and
private acquisition records out of the release.

The game supports a bounded evidentiary conclusion:

> THE FIRST TRAIL JOINED THE SECOND ROUTE.

It does not claim that transfer continuity proves a person's identity,
ownership, motive, or causation. The BRCG office-note discovery is a separate
lead and is not Euler case evidence.

# Player guide

## In the Records Office

Walk around, look at the objects, and talk to Arthur. The verb tray offers **GIVE, PICK UP, USE, OPEN, LOOK AT, PUSH, CLOSE, TALK TO, and PULL**. Your inventory holds collected items. USE and GIVE can pair an inventory item with a target; other verbs act on the selected object. An unsuccessful idea may earn a comment rather than advance the case.

Rook is learning the case and the office rules alongside you. No prior blockchain expertise, wallet, or API key is needed to play.

## At the terminal

Start in **CASE** for the next investigation question. Use **EXPLORE** to browse records or try another available question. **RESULTS** keeps a search’s matches, and **HISTORY** reopens searches you already ran.

Read the conditions before running a query. Select a matching record to inspect it; **MORE DETAILS** opens its retained fields and source information. Back controls return to your original record or list. Saving a clue, confirming evidence, saving the report, and closing the case are separate actions.

The [illustrated terminal guide](design/terminal-tour.md) explains those controls with non-solution examples. The [story page](story.md) introduces the characters without giving away the case.

## Sound and dialogue

Use the game’s displayed music and dialogue controls to adjust the experience. The office and terminal use different background tracks. The terminal’s magnet is a cosmetic degauss control; it is not a clue, and sound is not required to solve the investigation.

Dialogue advances with a click, `Space`, or `Enter`; `Escape` or right-click
cancels a selected command; and number keys `1`–`9` select verbs in display
order. Pointer, keyboard, emulated touch, and Pixel-sized mobile viewports are
covered by the release browser suite. The visible mobile full-screen shortcut
fits the complete game to the display when the browser grants full-screen mode.

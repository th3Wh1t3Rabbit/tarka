Limits and practical notes

Limits and practical notes

Tarka is a small competition-built point-and-click investigation, not a complete forensic platform or a promise that every blockchain incident can be converted into a polished mission. It uses a bounded historical collection; ordinary play does not fetch new provider data.

The optional BRCG detour uses a separate later snapshot. More elaborate false-lead journeys and additional playable missions are not promised parts of this release.

For what the records can and cannot establish, use Evidence limits. For how data was selected and compiled, use the collection method. These subjects have one home rather than a different disclaimer on every page.

The player guide covers interaction, sound and dialogue. The download guide separates the static browser archive from building the source. There are no signed native Windows or macOS applications. Browser saves do not migrate across origins, browsers, or devices.

Mobile browsers and operating systems may briefly delay animation frames under device pressure. The globe keeps one continuous driver across repeated input and preserves its direction, speed level, slowdown, and final state after a delay. Browser autoplay rules may keep music muted until the first gesture.

The production JavaScript bundle is large enough to produce Vite's chunk-size advisory, but the qualified build and cold load complete successfully. Reduced animation, dialogue display/speed controls, music mute, panning, pinch zoom, and fitted mobile full screen improve access but are not a formal accessibility conformance claim.
